How it works

How a very large model fits on one ordinary server.

The best AI models are too big for a single graphics card. That is why they usually need many. locus shares the work differently.

  1. 1

    The model lives in your server's memory.

    Your server already has far more memory than any graphics card. locus keeps most of the model there.

  2. 2

    One graphics card does the heavy part.

    The card takes the work that needs raw speed. Your server's own processor handles the rest.

  3. 3

    Your team just asks.

    People use it like any AI assistant. Their questions and your documents never leave your network.

Is it for you

A narrow job, done well.

locus is for one situation: you want a top-tier model in-house and you would rather not buy a room of new hardware. If that is not you, we will say so.

Good fit

locus makes sense when

  • You already own a large, recent server with plenty of memory.
  • You want one of the largest models, not a small one.
  • A team uses it through the working day. Answers come at a steady pace, not instantly.
  • Your data has to stay inside the company.
Not the right tool

Look elsewhere when

  • A smaller model is enough. It fits on one graphics card and runs far faster there on its own.
  • Thousands of people need instant answers at once. That still takes a rack of graphics cards.
